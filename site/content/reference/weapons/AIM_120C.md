The AIM-120C is the Advanced Medium-Range Air-to-Air Missile most Western fighters in
DCS carry. Its flight model has a single boost stage; compare its
[zero-lift drag](/compare/?s=weapons&f=flight.aerodynamics.cx0&r=AIM_120C,AIM_120) with
the AIM-120B to see the clipped-fin airframe.

DCS states its maximum range (`rangeKm`) separately from the AI's `Range_max`
(`flight.rangeMaxM`); neither is a launch envelope.
